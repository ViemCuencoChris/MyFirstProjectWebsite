The Midnight Velvet: Chocolate Fudgy Banana Cake

Where the nostalgia of home-baked banana bread meets the decadence of a Five-Star dessert. We start with over-ripened, caramelized bananas for a natural sweetness, then fold in a rich, 70% dark cocoa fudge. Each slice is dense, moist, and topped with a silky ganache that melts the moment it hits your tongue. It's not just a cake; it's the hug you didn't know you needed.

FROM A DUSTY APRON TO YOUR DAILY DOZEN

It started in a cramped apartment kitchen in 2022 with a single sourdough starter and a simple mission: to bring the soul back to the bread basket. What began as a personal quest for the perfect crust turned into a neighborhood obsession. We believe that time is the most important ingredient. That's why we still hand-knead our dough and let our batters rest, ensuring every bite of our Chocolate Fudgy Banana Cake or our signature sourdough tastes like a slow Sunday morning. We aren't just a shop; we're a tribute to the craft of traditional baking, right here in the heart of the community.
